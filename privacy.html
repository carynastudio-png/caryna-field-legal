Privacy Policy — Caryna Field - 4D Construction

Effective Date: November 2026

Developer: Caryna Studio (Nguyễn Trọng Gôn)

1. Offline-First Architecture & Local Storage

Caryna Field - 4D Construction is designed with an offline-first architecture. All construction schedules, zones, tasks, PDF/Excel imports, OCR scans, and project logs are stored locally on your device inside an encrypted/sandboxed database. We do not automatically upload your proprietary construction data to external servers.

2. Camera & File Access Permissions

The app requests access to your device camera solely for on-device optical character recognition (ML Kit OCR) and site documentation, and file storage access solely to import construction schedules or export user-initiated backups (.ssb) and reports (PDF/Word/Excel).

3. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

Pro subscriptions and lifetime licenses are processed securely through Google Play Billing. We do not collect or store your credit card or banking details.

For privacy inquiries, please contact: carynastudio@gmail.com
