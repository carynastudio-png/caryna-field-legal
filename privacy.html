Privacy Policy — Caryna Field - 4D Construction

Tiếng Việt ↓

Effective date: November 2026 · Developer: Caryna Studio (Nguyễn Trọng Gôn) · App: com.carynaphuquoc.carynafield

1. Offline-first architecture and local storage

Caryna Field is designed to work offline. Construction schedules, zones, tasks, PDF/Excel imports, OCR scans, signed audit-ledger entries and project logs are stored on your device in the app's private database. We do not automatically upload your construction data to external servers, and the app does not contain advertising or analytics SDKs.

2. Data and device permissions used

ItemWhat it is used forWhere it goes
Precise location (GPS)
ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION		 Optional and off by default. If you switch it on, the app reads your position only at the moment you sign an acceptance / audit entry (Ed25519 digital-signature ledger) and attaches it to that entry as evidence. The app never tracks your location in the background. Stored on your device in the project database with the signed entry. It is not sent to advertising, analytics or other third parties. If you use a workspace that synchronises with a server, signed entries of that workspace may be transmitted to that workspace's server. You can switch it off at any time in the app and in Android settings.
Google Sign-In Optional. When you choose to sign in with Google, the app receives only your email address, display name and Google account ID (scope email) to identify you; there is no server account behind it. You can instead use the app without any account ("use now without an account"). Kept in the app's encrypted secure storage on your device. Not used for advertising.
Biometric / device-credential check
USE_BIOMETRIC		 Optional confirmation that the person signing is present: before a digital signature is applied, the app asks Android to verify your fingerprint, face, or device PIN/pattern. This is handled entirely by the Android operating system. The app receives only a yes/no result and never receives or stores biometric data.
Notifications
POST_NOTIFICATIONS		 Local notifications on the device, for example schedule reminders and synchronisation status. This version does not use remote (push) notification services. Generated on the device; no notification content is sent to us.
Camera
CAMERA		 Only for on-device text recognition (ML Kit OCR) and site photo documentation. Processed on the device; photos stay in app storage until you export or delete them.
Files Only to import schedules you choose, and to export backups (.ssb) and reports (PDF/Word/Excel) you request, through the Android file picker / share sheet. You decide where exported files go.
Network state
INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE		 To check connectivity, reach Google Play Billing and Google Sign-In, and synchronise only if you use a server-backed workspace. —

3. In-app purchases and subscriptions

Pro subscriptions and lifetime licenses are processed by Google Play Billing. We do not collect or store your card or bank details. Purchase signatures are verified on your device.

4. Retention and deletion

Data stays on your device until you delete it. In the app, open Settings and choose to erase all local data (this removes projects, ledger, photos, documents, stored sign-in and keys), or clear storage / uninstall in Android settings. See the data deletion page.

5. Children

The app is intended for construction professionals and is not directed to children.

6. Changes and contact

We will update this page and its effective date when practices change. Privacy inquiries: carynastudio@gmail.com.
