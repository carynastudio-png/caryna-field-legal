Data Deletion Instructions — Caryna Field - 4D Construction

Caryna Field - 4D Construction stores all project schedules, tasks, and field records locally on your Android device rather than on central user-account servers.

How to Delete Your Data Immediately

  1. Open Caryna Field - 4D Construction on your device.
  2. Navigate to Settings and select the option to erase/clear all local application data, OR
  3. Open your Android device Settings > Apps > Caryna Field > Storage > Clear Storage / Uninstall.

All local project databases and cached files will be permanently removed from your device immediately. For any further assistance, contact carynastudio@gmail.com.
